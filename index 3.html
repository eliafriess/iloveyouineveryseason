For you, Seyn.

There’s so many words, yet I can’t seem to find anything that can describe what I feel for you. I love you seems like it’s not enough—it doesn’t capture the entirety of what I feel for you. Poetry can’t even describe how deep, and drowning this feeling is—and I don’t mean that in a bad way, because I know that even if I drown I would never dream or even think about wanting to rise for fresh air—as you are the only one I need to survive. I love you, more than words could ever describe, more than the feeling, more than the fights, more than the kisses, the hugs, and everything else. Because I love only you, with you everything doesn’t seem so bad. Happy 21st Birthday to you my wife, my home, my warmth, my person, the love of my life. I hope this day brings you joy, happiness, and warmth. May your day be as joyful as the color yellow, and not as blue as your favorite color. And once you figure out the dreams you’d like to achieve, may you achieve them with me beside you, I hope your wishes do come true if you have any. Thank you for the joy, warmth, peace, love, and everything that you bring and spread every day. I hope when you change and grow into the best, and better version of yourself I’m still by your side, may you continue to spread the joys and kindness your heart has. 사랑해요 🫶 I love you until the sun loses its light, until the stars stops shining, and until the moon rests its light. Thank you for existing, for your life, for your patience, for loving me. Enjoy your morning, and the rest of the day. 🫶
